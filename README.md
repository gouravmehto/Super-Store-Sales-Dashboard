📊  Task 8 - Super Store Sales Dashboard | Power Bi
----------------------------------------------------------------------
An interactive Power BI dashboard that visualizes and analyzes sales data from a fictional Super Store. The report covers sales performance, profit trends, regional insights, category breakdowns, and short-term forecasting.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🧭 Navigation Overview

📄 Page 1: Sales Summary

Provides an executive overview of the company’s performance:

- Key Metrics:

     - Total Sales: 1.6M

    - Quantity Sold: 22K

    - Profit: 175K

    - Average Delivery Time: 4 days

- Visualizations:

   - 📊 Sales by Payment Mode (COD 43%, Online 35%, Cards 22%)

   - 🗺️ Sales & Profit by U.S. State (Map)

  - 📉 Monthly trends for Sales & Profit (2019–2020)

  - 📦 Sales by Category & Sub-Category (Treemap)

  - 🚚 Sales by Ship Mode (Standard, Second, First, Same Day)

  - 🧩 Sales Breakdown by Region and Segment

📈 Page 2: Forecasting & Advanced Analysis

Enables predictive insights and deeper sub-category analysis:

- 15-Day Forecast:

    - Based on historical trends

    - Dual views: full time-series and zoomed-in recent trend

- Sales Growth by Category:

   - Waterfall chart showing YoY performance (2019 vs 2020)

- Sub-Category Table:

   - Orders, Total Sales, and Profit with color-coded performance

   -  Highlights loss-generating segments (e.g., Tables with negative profit)
     
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

🔍 Key Insights

  - Top Selling Sub-Categories: Phones (196K), Chairs (181K), Binders (174K)

 - Most Profitable: Accessories, Phones, Chairs

  - Least Profitable: Tables (negative profit)

  - Dominant Region: West (33% of sales)

  - Segment Leader: Consumer (48% of total sales)

 - Preferred Ship Mode: Standard Class (0.33M in sales)
